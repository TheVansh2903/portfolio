Projects

1 - BookWise:

BookWise is an AI-powered financial management and consulting platform designed to help businesses understand, manage, and improve their finances.

The vision is to create an intelligent financial agent that can handle routine financial tasks, analyze business data, provide actionable insights, and act as a virtual financial consultant. Businesses can choose between an AI-powered consultant or a human financial consultant based on the service package they select.

The platform aims to bring accounting, financial management, automation, analytics, and intelligent business recommendations together in one system.

Key Focus Area

  • 🤖 AI-powered financial assistance
  • 📊 Business financial analytics and insights
  • 💰 Accounting and financial management
  • ⚡ Automation of repetitive financial tasks
  • 👨‍💼 AI and human consultant options
  • 📈 Business performance insights
  • 🔐 Secure user and business data management
